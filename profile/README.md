# Faxioms

### Clarity for every financial decision.

**Faxioms (Financial Axioms)** is building a comprehensive financial information and research platform to make India's financial landscape more accessible, transparent, and understandable.

Our mission is to organise fragmented financial information, simplify financial research, and help individuals make informed decisions across every stage of their financial journey.

## What We're Building

Faxioms aims to make financial information easier to discover, understand, compare, and use.

### Financial Markets & Investment Research

Our platform currently focuses on providing accessible financial data, research, and insights across:

- **Indian Stock Markets:** NSE and BSE listed companies, company financials, quarterly results, technical analysis, and business research.
- **Mutual Funds & ETFs:** Fund performance, portfolio holdings, investment strategies, and comparisons.
- **Initial Public Offerings (IPOs):** Company fundamentals, financial disclosures, risk factors, and public issue information.
- **Institutional Investments:** FII, FPI, and DII activity, institutional fund flows, and market positioning.
- **Corporate Ownership:** Shareholding patterns, institutional ownership, and ownership trends.
- **Market Intelligence:** Market activity, company announcements, financial disclosures, and investment research.

### Expanding Financial Accessibility

Our long-term vision extends beyond investing to the broader Indian financial ecosystem, including:

- Savings accounts and fixed deposits
- Government bonds and small savings schemes
- PPF, EPF, NPS, pensions, and retirement planning
- Commodities and international investments
- Financial product discovery, education, and comparison
- Multilingual financial information across Indian languages
- Personalized financial guidance and research tools

Our objective is not simply to aggregate more financial data, but to make financial products understandable through clear explanations of their benefits, risks, costs, and alternatives.
